-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. INDUSTRY CONTEXT & BACKGROUND
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The domestic labor sector in India (maids, cooks, nannies) represents one of 
the largest unorganized informal markets in the country.

- Market Size      : Estimated at over $10 Billion USD.
- Informal Share   : Over 90% of hiring relies on informal word-of-mouth or 
                     predatory local placement agencies.
- Infrastructure   : Severe absence of transparent, technology-driven trust 
                     platforms for domestic household services.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. CORE EMPLOYER PAIN POINTS (DEMAND-SIDE)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Trust & Security Gap in Gated Communities
    - Gated apartment complexes enforce strict gate access. Unverified workers 
      present safety and theft risks for residents.
    - Conducting police checks or background verification independently is 
      complex and time-consuming for individual households.

2.2 Time-Slot Incompatibility & High Friction
    - Household help is required during strict time windows (e.g. 7:00-8:30 AM).
    - Traditional hiring provides no visibility into a worker's daily schedule, 
      causing schedule clashes and high turnover.

2.3 Middleman Exploitation & High Costs
    - Unregulated placement agencies charge employers heavy upfront fees 
      (1-2 months salary) without retention guarantees or background checks.

2.4 Skill & Language Mismatch
    - Specific household needs (dietary preferences, infant care) are rarely 
      matched accurately, causing repeated hiring failures.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. CORE DOMESTIC WORKER PAIN POINTS (SUPPLY-SIDE)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Predatory Agent Cut
    - Unregulated placement agencies demand upfront registration fees from 
      workers or deduct ongoing cuts from monthly earnings.

3.2 Digital & Language Barriers
    - Standard hiring apps are built in English for white-collar jobs. Domestic 
      workers face literacy and digital navigation hurdles.

3.3 Absence of Portable Work Identity
    - Workers build years of reliable work experience but lack verifiable 
      records or digital profiles to prove credentials to new employers.

3.4 Wage Instability & Unclear Hours
    - Lack of transparent wage benchmarking leaves workers vulnerable to 
      underpayment and unpredictable working hours.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. THE SEVIKAA SOLUTION & ARCHITECTURAL INNOVATIONS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 Curated Trust Architecture
    - Every profile undergoes mandatory verification (Aadhaar, Police clearance 
      status, Tele-interview, and Video Intro audit) before going live.

4.2 Society-Centric Hyper-Local Matching
    - Gated societies are structured as database entities. Workers are matched 
      with employers in their immediate apartment complex or neighborhood.

4.3 Granular Slot Availability Engine
    - Workers select precise weekly availability, enabling employers to filter 
      specifically for candidates matching their daily routine.

4.4 100% Free Platform for Workers
    - Sevikaa strictly charges ZERO fees to workers. Revenue relies entirely 
      on transparent employer subscriptions for unlocking contact details.

4.5 Multilingual Accessibility & Tele-Onboarding
    - Support for 8 Indian regional languages + phone-guided tele-onboarding 
      ensures accessibility for workers of all literacy levels.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. SOCIO-ECONOMIC IMPACT & VISION
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Women Empowerment     : Direct employment access and wage transparency for 
                           female domestic workers.
2. Labor Formalization    : Building verifiable digital work identities for 
                           unorganized informal workers.
3. Community Security     : Pre-verified workforce access for gated society 
                           residents and security committees.
4. Scalable Growth Vision : Repeatable digital blueprint scaling across urban 
                           metropolitan centers in India.
